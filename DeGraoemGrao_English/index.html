About us:

At 2024, Julciléia Carla create the "De Grão em Grão", a brand specialized in release of seasonings, grains and herbs of quality. With a objective of serving family mothers who seek fresh and reliable ingredients.

"De Grão em Grão" makes it easy to prepare healthy and tasty meals, offering a selection of products that make everyday life more practical and enjoyable. With a focus on quality and convenience, the brand stands out for its commitment to helping families enrich their daily meals.

Futhermore, "De Grão em Grão" stands out for its resale approach, collaborating with local produtors who guarantee the quality and freshness of seasonings, grains and herbs. By prioritizing partnerships with small farmers, the brand not only offers excellent products, but also supports the local economy, promoting sustainable practices that benefit the entire community. That philosophy allows mothers to have access to reliable ingredients, enriching your meals with flavor and health.
